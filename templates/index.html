Cyber Awareness Curriculum

Core principles explained in simple English and Hinglish to protect yourself, your family, and your peers.

1. Phishing & Smishing Anatomy

Attackers exploit 4 emotional triggers: Fear, Urgency, Greed, and Authority. If a message urges you to act within minutes or lose access, pause immediately.

Hinglish Guide: "Jab bhi koi email ya SMS kahe ki 'Aapka account 24 ghante mein band ho jayega', toh 99% yeh scam hota hai. Real banks kabhi achanak block karne ki dhamki nahi dete."
  • Always inspect full sender email address, not just display name
  • Beware of greetings like "Dear Customer" instead of your actual name

2. The Golden Rule of OTP & 2FA

An OTP (One-Time Password) is a secondary digital key specifically designed for your eyes only. Legitimate banks and portals will NEVER ask for it.

Hinglish Guide: "OTP ka matlab hai One Time Password. Yeh aapki tijori ki chabhi hai. Bank manager, police, ya customer care bolkar phone karne wale ko OTP dena matlab apne paise unhe saumpna."
  • Read the SMS carefully: it states what the OTP is authorizing (e.g. 'Debiting ₹10,000')
  • Enable Authenticator Apps (TOTP) instead of SMS OTP where possible

3. Spotting Lookalike URLs (Typosquatting)

Scammers register domain names that look nearly identical to popular services by substituting characters (0 for O, 1 for l) or using deceptive subdomains.

Hinglish Guide: "Hamesha domain ka aakhri hissa dekhein. Agar link hai `netflix.account-billing.xyz`, toh main domain `account-billing.xyz` hai, Netflix nahi!"
  • Check domain root before the first slash: e.g. `google.com` vs `google-login.top`
  • Bookmark important banking and portal sites instead of searching on Google

4. Social Engineering & Fake Authority

Cybercriminals impersonate police, customs officials (Digital Arrest), or IT support. They isolate victims and demand confidentiality.

Hinglish Guide: "'Digital Arrest' naam ka koi kanuni procedure nahi hota! Police ya CBI kabhi video call par paise transfer karke case band karne ko nahi bolti."
  • Consult family or call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline in India)
  • Never install remote desktop tools (AnyDesk, TeamViewer) on stranger's advice

Privacy-First Architecture

How CyberShield AI protects user privacy while evaluating threats with Gemini.

Zero Persistent Storage

CyberShield AI operates completely statelessly. User messages, screenshots, and logs are analyzed in ephemeral RAM and never written to a disk database or telemetry store.

Active PII Masking Engine

Before text reaches Google Gemini, our built-in regex sanitization scans and redacts OTPs, credit card numbers, passwords, and phone numbers with placeholders like [REDACTED_OTP].

Hybrid Intelligence Architecture

We combine deterministic rule-based heuristics (raw IP addresses, high-risk TLDs, keyword triggers) with Gemini 2.5 Flash's deep semantic reasoning. If offline, the local heuristic engine takes over seamlessly.

Ethical AI Limitations Disclaimer

AI heuristics are assistive tools for awareness, not infallible enterprise firewalls. Never open an unverified attachment or send funds based solely on an automated heuristic score.
